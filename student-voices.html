Life in UP8 & Extracurriculars

📍 Campus & Transportation

Located at Saint-Denis (Metro Line 13). The campus is huge, with a great library (BU) perfect for group projects, and plenty of CROUS cafeterias for cheap student meals.

🎉 Associations

Join the digital and cultural associations on campus! It's the best way to network, build your soft skills, and blow off some steam after coding sessions.
