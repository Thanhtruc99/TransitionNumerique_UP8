Life in UP8 (Paris 8 Campus)

📍 Location & Transport: We are located at Saint-Denis. Just hop on Metro Line 13 (University stop) and you are right at the campus gates.

📚 The Library (BU): The university library is huge and modern. It’s the perfect spot to book a private room and grind on your group projects with your TN teammates.

☕ Food & Chill: There are plenty of CROUS cafeterias around for a cheap, hot 3.30€ student meal. Great coffee spots nearby for when you need a break from coding.

Student Associations (BDE)

🎉 Work Hard, Play Hard

Don't forget to join the campus associations! Whether you are into e-sports, digital arts, or just want to attend the BDE (Student Bureau) networking parties, getting involved is the best way to build your soft skills and blow off some steam after intensive seminars.
