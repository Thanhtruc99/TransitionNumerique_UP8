📚 Academics

Cross-disciplinary courses & Project-based teamwork.

What You Will Learn

Forget boring, single-track degrees. Our curriculum is a unique blend of Tech, Humanities, and Business. Here is the core breakdown:

💻 Tech & Data

Learn the languages of the digital world. You will dive into Web Development, Data Analysis, and Information Architecture. We don't just teach you how to code; we teach you how to understand digital monitoring on an international scale.

🧠 Humanities & Society

This is what makes our Master unique. We study the impact of technology through the lens of Psychology, Sociology, Law, and Economics. You will learn to design digital solutions that are ethical and human-centered.

💼 Professional Skills

Get ready for the real world. You will master Digital Project Management, UX/UI evaluation, and strategic planning, heavily supported by our partnerships like the Cap Digital network.

Learning Methods: Teamwork over Theory

If you are looking to sit silently in a lecture hall taking notes for two years, this program isn't for you. At Master TN, we believe in Learning by Doing.

  • 100% Project-Based: Every semester, you will work on real-world case studies. You will build apps, design digital strategies, and solve problems for actual clients or startups.
  • Peer-to-Peer Learning: Our cohorts are diverse. You might team up with a coding expert, a law graduate, and a psychology major. The IT students help the Humanities students with coding, and the Humanities students guide the project's ethical and social strategy. We guide each other!
  • Intensive Seminars: Instead of traditional weekly classes, we often run intensive multi-day seminars led by industry professionals, researchers, and entrepreneurs.

💡 Student Voice: "The group projects can be intense, but they perfectly mimic a real tech company environment. You learn how to communicate your ideas to people who don't have the same academic background as you. That skill alone got me my current job." – Alex, M2 Student
