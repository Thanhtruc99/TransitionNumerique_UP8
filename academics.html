📚 Academics

Cross-disciplinary courses & Project-based teamwork.

Our Four Pillars of Expertise

The program is built around four distinct specializations that interact constantly, mimicking a real-world digital agency or tech department.

01

Digital Projects and Uses

Focus on managing the lifecycle of digital products with a user-centric mindset. Learn to lead transitions from ideation to deployment.

02

Computer Science & Info Processing

Master the technical backbone of the digital world, from data analysis and information systems to technical architecture.

03

Digital Communication

Learn to craft powerful narratives and visual strategies to bring digital innovations to light and engage global audiences.

04

Digital Law

Navigate the complex legal landscape of data privacy (GDPR), intellectual property, and digital ethics in a connected world.

Learning Methods: Teamwork over Theory

At Master TN, we believe in Learning by Doing.

  • 100% Project-Based: Every semester, you will work on real-world case studies for actual clients or startups.
  • Peer-to-Peer Learning: IT students help Humanities students with coding, and Humanities students guide the ethical strategy. We guide each other!
