Life at Paris 8 (UP8) Campus

📍 Location & Transport

Located in Saint-Denis, the campus is super accessible. Just hop on Metro Line 13 (Saint-Denis - Université stop) and you are right at the campus gates. No long walks needed!

📚 The Library (BU)

The massive University Library (Bibliothèque Universitaire) is the heart of our study life. It’s the perfect spot to book a private workroom and grind on your group projects with your TN teammates.

☕ Food & Chill

Need a break from coding or reading? There are plenty of CROUS cafeterias around for a hot, affordable student meal (around 3.30€), plus great coffee spots to relax between classes.

Extracurricular Activities & Associations

🎉 Work Hard, Play Hard

University isn't just about studying. We strongly encourage you to join the campus associations! Whether you are into digital arts, e-sports, or social initiatives, getting involved is the best way to network, build soft skills, and blow off some steam after intensive seminars.

Connect with the BDE

The Student Bureau (BDE) organizes parties, integration weekends (WEI), and networking events throughout the year. It's the easiest way to meet students from other master's programs.
