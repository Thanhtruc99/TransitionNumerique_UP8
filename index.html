🚀 About Master TN: Beyond the Hype

In partnership with CFA AFIA, the Master in Digital Transitions (TN) is not your average tech degree. We go beyond the media hype to help you turn complex data into real-world social and economic innovations. Designed by blending multiple disciplines, this program gives you a true competitive edge to lead in today's fast-paced digital landscape.

✨ What Makes This Master Different?

Why you should join us?

🧠

Cross-Disciplinary Expertise

Forget boundaries. We blend IT, Psychology, Law, Economics, and Humanities. Instead of just learning to code, you build a versatile, future-proof skill set that adapts to whatever the tech industry needs next.

📊

Real Data, Zero Fluff

Look past fleeting tech trends. Our core focus is on critical analysis and international digital monitoring. We teach you to base your projects and decisions on solid, scientific data rather than temporary hype.

🤝

Industry Immersion

Learn from the insiders. Through intensive annual seminars, you'll work directly with researchers, entrepreneurs, and experts from the Cap Digital network to bring your digital innovations to life.
