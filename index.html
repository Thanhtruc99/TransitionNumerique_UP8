All you need to know about Master TN at Paris 8

Master TN – Frequently Asked Questions

How is the "Project Management" focus integrated into the curriculum? ⌄

The entire program is structured around the digital project lifecycle. You will work in teams to manage real-world cases, from initial data analysis to final product delivery and communication.

Will I learn about the legal and ethical sides of digital projects? ⌄

Yes. We offer a specific Specialization in Digital Law, ensuring you understand data privacy (GDPR) and intellectual property, which are crucial for any project manager today.

What "Digital Storytelling" skills will I acquire? ⌄

You will learn how to translate complex data into engaging stories using visual tools and narrative techniques to effectively pitch your projects to stakeholders.

Can I work on actual professional projects during my studies? ⌄

Absolutely. Through our partnership with the Cap Digital network and intensive seminars, you will collaborate directly with entrepreneurs and researchers on live innovations.

Do I need to be a coding expert to join? ⌄

No. We welcome students from Humanities, Law, and Tech. We provide "Mise à niveau" (Leveling) courses in Semester 1 to ensure everyone has a solid foundation in digital tools.
