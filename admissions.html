🎓 Admissions

Process, Requirements, and Timeline to join us.

Requirements (Who can apply?)

We welcome diverse backgrounds! You need a Bachelor's degree (Licence 3) in fields like Humanities, IT, Communications, Sociology, or Economics. Most importantly, you need a strong motivation for the digital transition.

Admission Timeline & Process

1

Prepare Dossier

Get your CV, Cover Letter (Lettre de motivation), and transcripts ready. Authentic voices win!

2

Submit on MonMaster

Apply through the national MonMaster platform before the official deadline.

3

The Interview

Shortlisted candidates will have a short, friendly chat with the jury about their career goals.

4

Alternance Contract

Sign your apprenticeship contract with CFA AFIA and officially join the Master TN family.
