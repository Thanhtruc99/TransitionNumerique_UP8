🎓 Admissions

Clear, concise, and straightforward. Here is how you join us.

Admission Requirements

We welcome diverse backgrounds in Tech, Communications, and Social Sciences. Choose your level to see specific requirements.

  • Master 1 (M1)
  • Master 2 (M2)
  • 🎓 Degree: Bachelor's degree (Licence - 180 ECTS) in IT, Communications, Social Sciences, or Management.
  • 💻 Experience: Personal digital projects or practical tech experience are highly valued.
  • 🔄 Professionals: No Bachelor's? You can apply via the VAPP process if you have significant relevant work experience.
  • 🎓 Degree: Completed Master 1 (60 ECTS) in a related field (Digital Design, Project Management, Digital Humanities).
  • 🎯 Project Focus: A highly defined career or research project aligned with Digital Transitions.
  • 💼 Alternance Rule: You must secure a company contract (Apprentissage/Professionnalisation) for official enrollment in the Alternance track.

Process & Timeline

Applying for Master 1

All candidates must apply through the national platform.

Platform: MonMaster.gouv.fr

📅 Timeline: March - April

Applying for Master 2

Candidates apply directly via the university's internal system.

Platform: e-Candidat (Paris 8)

📅 Timeline: March - May

Special Tracks Info

💼 Alternance (Work-Study)

Your tuition fees are fully covered by your company or OPCO! For administrative help regarding your contract, contact the Pôle Alternance early at: alternance@univ-paris8.fr.

📈 Formation Continue (Working Professionals)

Returning to school? You can finance your degree using your CPF (Compte Personnel de Formation) or employer funding. Contact the Service Formation Continue to set up your financial dossier.
