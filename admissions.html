🎓 Admissions

No stress. Here is your roadmap to join the Master TN.

Who Can Apply? (Requirements)

We don't just look for one specific profile. The digital transition needs diverse minds!

Academic Background: You need a Bachelor's degree (Licence 3 or equivalent) in fields such as Humanities, Information Technology (IT), Communications, Sociology, or Economics.

The X-Factor: What we really look for is a strong, genuine motivation for the digital world and a clear vision of your professional project. Show us your passion!

Application Process & Timeline

1

Prepare Dossier

Get your CV and Cover Letter ready. Make it authentic! Don't use generic templates.

2

Submit on MonMaster

Upload your application via the national MonMaster platform (March - April).

3

The Interview

If shortlisted, you will be invited for a 15-minute chat with our jury to discuss your goals.

4

Find an Alternance

Once accepted, start looking for your work-study contract with the help of CFA AFIA.
