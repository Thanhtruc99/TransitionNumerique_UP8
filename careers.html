💡 Internship & Alternance Tips from Seniors

  • Start early: Don't wait until September. Start looking for your alternance in April!
  • Network on LinkedIn: Connect with Master TN Alumni. We are always happy to refer you.
  • Build a Portfolio: Show your class projects. Practical work speaks louder than just grades.
