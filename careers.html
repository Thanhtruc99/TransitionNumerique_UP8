Internship & Alternance Tips

💡 Top Advice from Senior Students

  • Start early: Don't wait until September. Start looking for your alternance as early as April!
  • Optimize LinkedIn: Recruiters look at your profile. Add "Master TN - Paris 8" to your headline.
  • Build a Portfolio: Show your class projects (websites, case studies). Practical work speaks louder than just a CV.
  • Leverage the Network: Reach out to Master TN Alumni. We are always happy to refer you or review your resume!
