Work-Study & Further Steps

💡 Alternance (Work-Study) Tips

Finding an apprenticeship is a crucial step. Use our network! The CFA AFIA is there to match your professional profile with the right company. Start your search early and tailor your CV to highlight both your technical skills and your humanities background.

🎓 Want to go further? (PhD)

Not ready to leave the academic world? Students who wish to dive deeper into research focusing on ICT (Information and Communication Technologies) and user behavior can continue their studies in a Doctoral Program (PhD) after graduating from Master TN.
